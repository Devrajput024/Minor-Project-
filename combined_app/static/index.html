RESEARCH & METHODOLOGY

Evidence with context.
Limitations in view.

Two independent model workflows. Separate datasets, targets and evaluation histories.

01 / IMAGING

Supported inference

Ultrasound and histopathology use preserved ResNet-18 checkpoints. Modality verification gates classifier access; chest X-ray remains excluded from live inference.

02 / MULTI-OMICS

Recorded status task

The molecular pipeline predicts Dataset C recorded vital status labels: 0 = Alive and 1 = Deceased. It does not predict survival time.

03 / INTERPRETATION

Research use only

These independent analyses are exploratory and are not clinically validated. Outputs are not medical advice or a clinical diagnosis.
